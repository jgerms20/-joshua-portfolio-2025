01 — The Setup

A Different Season

"Baseball Is Something Else" ran all summer. It was built to make the regular season feel worth your Tuesday night — quirky, warm, a little strange.

The postseason needed a different register. October baseball isn't charming. It's where a career turns on one at-bat, and where players nobody outside their city could name become household names in a week.

02 — The Idea

Where Major Is Made

The name says it. "Major" sits inside Major League Baseball, and October is when the word earns itself.

The campaign framed the postseason as the proving ground — the stage that separates a good season from a permanent piece of baseball history.

03 — My Role

My Role

As Brand Strategist at Wieden+Kennedy Portland, I worked on the strategic foundation for the postseason platform and how it carried across the run — from Wild Card through the World Series.

The strategic problem was continuity without repetition: the postseason had to feel like the same brand as the summer campaign while carrying completely different stakes.
