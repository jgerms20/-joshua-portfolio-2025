# Brand Voice — Analyze or Write in Any Brand's Voice

Analyzes a brand's voice, or writes new copy matching that brand's style.

## Usage
`/brand-voice [brand]` — e.g., `/brand-voice Nike` or `/brand-voice Gatorade`

## Instructions

### Step 1: Identify the task
Two modes:
- **Analyze**: "Describe [brand]'s voice so I can write in it"
- **Write**: "Write [content type] for [brand] in their voice"

If unclear, ask which mode.

### Step 2a: Analyze mode — Voice breakdown
```
## BRAND VOICE — [Brand]

### What they sound like
[3-4 adjectives, then explain]

### What they never say
[List of language, tones, or approaches this brand avoids]

### The brand's belief system
[What they fundamentally believe about their customer / the world]

### Vocabulary
Words they use: [list]
Words they avoid: [list]
Sentence length: [short punchy / long narrative / mix]

### Tone by context
- Social: [how they sound on social]
- OOH/broadcast: [how they sound in paid media]
- Long-form: [how they sound in essays or reports]

### Examples
[2-3 example lines that nail their voice]

### How to write in this voice
[3-5 rules for getting it right]
```

### Step 2b: Write mode
Use the voice framework to write the requested content. For brands Joshua has worked with (Gatorade, DIRECTV, BMW, Nike, etc.) — use the actual campaign context from his portfolio case studies.

### Step 3: Offer to add to portfolio
If new copy was written, ask: "Want to use this in your Gatorade/DIRECTV/[brand] case study?"
