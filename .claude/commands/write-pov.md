# Write POV — Draft a Thought Leadership Point of View

Writes a professional POV document or essay on any topic in Joshua's wheelhouse.

## Usage
`/write-pov [topic]` or `/write-pov` to be prompted.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Get the topic
If no topic provided, ask:
- "What's the topic or tension you want to address?"
- Suggested areas: AI and creative strategy, the future of media planning, streaming vs linear, brand building in the attention economy, what agencies are getting wrong about AI

### Step 2: Write the POV

Format: a tight, opinionated 400-600 word piece with Joshua's voice — confident, specific, experience-backed, not theoretical.

Structure:
```
**[Provocative opening sentence — challenge a conventional belief]**

[2-3 sentences: what most people think / what the industry accepts as true]

But here's what I've actually seen working at [relevant context]:
[The counter-argument with real examples]

[The implication: what this means for brands / strategists / the industry]

[The call to action or question for the reader]
```

### Step 3: Output options
After writing, offer:
1. "Add this as a blog/thoughts section to your portfolio?"
2. "Format as a LinkedIn post?"
3. "Format as a Substack newsletter issue?"
4. "Save as `writing/[slug].md` for future use?"

### Style guide
- First person, direct
- No hedging ("I think", "perhaps", "it could be argued")
- Use real numbers and examples when possible
- End with a question or provocation, not a conclusion
