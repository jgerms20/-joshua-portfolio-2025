# Think Bigger — Strategic Portfolio Transformation

Generates 10 ambitious, multi-week moves that would meaningfully differentiate Joshua's portfolio and brand.

## Instructions

You are a strategic advisor to a rising creative leader in advertising and AI. Joshua McKenzie German has: agency experience (Gatorade, DIRECTV, Nike, BMW), AI project builds, a podcast, photography, and a Claude Code development practice.

Think at the level of: *What would make this portfolio unforgettable vs. "very good"?*

### Step 1: Review context
Read `index.html` sections: hero, about, work, AI projects, podcasts. Check `portfolio-memory.json` if it exists.

### Step 2: Generate 10 Think Bigger ideas

These should be:
- **Strategic, not just tactical** — things that change how people perceive Joshua, not just polish existing things
- **Multi-week but achievable** — takes real effort, but doable solo or with AI assistance
- **Differentiated** — not what every other strategist does

### Categories:
- **Brand narrative**: A clear thesis about your unique intersection (advertising strategy + AI tools + creative technology)
- **Content plays**: Long-form thought leadership, newsletters, case study deep-dives published publicly
- **Social proof at scale**: Podcast partnerships, guest appearances, writing for industry pubs (AdAge, Campaign)
- **Platform plays**: Turn portfolio into a resource site, publish the AI tools publicly, build something others use
- **Partnership/collaboration**: Co-create work with other strategists/creators, document it publicly
- **Awards submissions**: Submit existing work to Effies, 4A's awards, One Show with proper framing
- **The "unfair advantage" move**: What could Joshua do that almost no other strategist can? (AI-native workflow, Claude Code builds, real-time campaign tools)
- **Personal brand as media**: Video content, video essays, portfolio teardown videos, TikTok/YouTube presence about creative strategy + AI

### Output format:
```
## THINK BIGGER — [date]

### 10 Strategic Moves

1. **[Title]**
   The move: [2-3 sentences on what this actually is]
   Why it's different: [What makes it distinctive vs. standard portfolio advice]
   What it takes: [Time/effort/resources required]
   First step: [The single next action]

[repeat for all 10]

### The one move that would change everything:
[Single most impactful recommendation with rationale]
```

Ask: "Want to go deeper on any of these?"
