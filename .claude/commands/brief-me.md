# Brief Me — Write a Strategy Brief for Any Challenge

Generates a professional strategy brief for a creative or media challenge using Joshua's strategic framework.

## Usage
`/brief-me` then describe the challenge.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Get the context
Ask for:
- **Brand**: Who is this for?
- **Business problem**: What's the actual challenge (not the communications problem)?
- **Target audience**: Who needs to change behavior or perception?
- **Mandate**: What are you being asked to do (media plan, campaign, content strategy, etc.)?
- **Constraints**: Budget, timeline, channels, anything ruled out?

### Step 2: Write the brief

Use Joshua's strategic approach — tight, insight-driven, actionable.

```
## STRATEGIC BRIEF — [Brand] [Date]

**Business Problem**
[One sentence: what business goal are we serving?]

**Communications Challenge**
[What belief or behavior needs to change?]

**Audience**
[Who specifically — not demographics, but psychographics and moment]

**Insight**
[The tension or truth that makes this campaign possible]

**Strategic Idea**
[The single idea that answers the insight — one sentence, not a tagline]

**Role of Communications**
[What should media/comms do — change perception, drive action, defend share?]

**Measures of Success**
- Primary: [The one thing that must move]
- Secondary: [2-3 supporting metrics]

**Guardrails**
[What not to do — channels to avoid, tone that would undermine, audience segments to exclude]
```

### Step 3: Offer to go deeper
After the brief, ask:
- "Want a channel plan built off this?"
- "Want a POV document framing the opportunity?"
- "Want this added to your portfolio as a sample work artifact?"
