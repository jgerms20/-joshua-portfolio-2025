# Think Big — Portfolio Level-Up Ideas

Generates 10 high-impact, realistic improvements to the portfolio — things Joshua could add or upgrade right now.

## Instructions

You are a senior creative director and UX strategist reviewing Joshua McKenzie German's portfolio at `/home/user/-joshua-portfolio-2026/`.

### Step 1: Review what exists
Read `index.html` briefly — specifically the nav, the about/hero copy, the work section, AI projects, and the footer. Read `portfolio-memory.json` if it exists.

### Step 2: Generate 10 Think Big ideas

Each idea should be:
- **Specific and actionable** — not "improve copy" but "rewrite the hero headline to lead with your Nike/Gatorade credibility"
- **Realistic** — things you could actually build or write today
- **High ROI** — things a recruiter or creative director would notice

### Categories to pull from:
- **New case studies to add**: Social media campaigns, content strategy work, consulting projects, side hustles, school projects worth showing
- **Portfolio copy upgrades**: Sharper headline, more specific results, better verbs
- **Missing credentials**: Awards, certifications, speaking, press, recognition
- **Features to build**: Password-protected work, PDF download, video reel, recruiter-friendly one-pager
- **Language leveling**: Remove weak phrases, add specificity, quantify more
- **New sections**: Recommendations/testimonials, skills matrix, "What I'm thinking about" blog, reading list
- **Visual upgrades**: Hero reel video, before/after campaign results, data visualizations of results

### Output format:
```
## THINK BIG — [date]

### 10 Upgrades Worth Making

1. **[Short title]**
   What: [1-2 sentences on what it is]
   Why: [Why it matters to someone evaluating you]
   How: [Specific next step to start it]

[repeat for all 10]

### Quick wins (can do today, <30 min each):
- [3-4 small things]
```

### After the list
Ask: "Want me to start on any of these?"
